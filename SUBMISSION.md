Title: Alt Text Check

Notes / Description:
Built a reusable, role-separated alt-text workflow. A publisher freezes visible facts and a no-inference rule, a designated contributor submits a versioned draft, and GenLayer validators return a fixed-order fact mask plus bounded accessibility label. A separate reviewer alone requests revision, approves, or rejects. The contract never claims to inspect pixels or infer identity, health, emotion, intent, or other private traits.
